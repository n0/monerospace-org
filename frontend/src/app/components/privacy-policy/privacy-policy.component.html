Privacy Policy

Updated: May 16, 2026


Use Your Own Node When Privacy Matters

MoneroSpace displays public Monero chain and mempool data from the daemon configured by the site operator. For the strongest privacy, run your own Monero node and your own MoneroSpace instance instead of relying on a public explorer.


What This Explorer Can See

Regular page and API requests reveal standard web metadata to the operator, including your IP address, user agent, requested URL, and request time. Operators may keep server logs for reliability, abuse prevention, debugging, and capacity planning.


What Monero Hides

Monero's RingCT and stealth-address design means this site cannot determine transaction recipients or transferred amounts from public chain data. MoneroSpace does not build address histories, wallet histories, account pages, or public spent/unspent output claims.


Proof Verification

The tx_proof verification form sends the transaction hash, recipient address, and proof signature to this instance's backend. A tx_proof signature is intended to be shared; private view keys, spend keys, seed phrases, and tx secret keys should not be sent to this server.


Local Preferences

The frontend may store local preferences such as language, theme, fiat currency, and timezone in your browser. These preferences are used to render the interface and are not needed for chain analysis.


Third Parties

This instance may call configured upstream services such as a Monero daemon, fiat price provider, or wallet-RPC verifier. Public deployments should document their own logging, analytics, proxy, and retention policies alongside this baseline policy.
